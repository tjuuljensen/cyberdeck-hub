## Included country-specific sources

The English profile includes four St John Ambulance short first-aid guides and one Scout Adventures knots handout because they cover core reference topics. Their titles and destination filenames identify them as UK sources; they are supplemental and do not replace local emergency guidance. The Denmark map asset is English-language and is labeled accordingly.
