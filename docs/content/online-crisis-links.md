# Online crisis links

Source review: 2026-10-25. This is a small link directory for a Danish
household. It is not an alert channel, an authoritative incident assessment,
or a substitute for local emergency instructions. The Hub does not embed,
mirror, or redistribute the linked services.

The home page shows the group only when the Hub's existing online-catalog
connectivity probe succeeds and the browser reports an online connection. The
check is a reachability hint, not a guarantee that every listed service is
reachable. If the group is absent, use local references and radio; do not wait
for an online service during an emergency.

| Service | Link | Use and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World Monitor | <https://www.worldmonitor.app/> | User-suggested global context dashboard. It is a third-party service, not a government alert channel. |
| DR Nyheder | <https://www.dr.dk/nyheder> | Danish public-service news. Requires internet. |
| DR Lyd and P4 | <https://www.dr.dk/lyd> | Danish public-service radio streaming. Streaming requires internet; use a battery-powered FM radio if networks or power fail. |
| TV 2 Nyheder | <https://nyheder.tv2.dk/> | Danish news. Requires internet. |
| Politi Update | <https://politi.dk/faa-politi-update-som-rssfeed> | Danish police operational updates, available through RSS; the built-in reader offers the national feed and individual police districts. |

World Monitor's official site describes its product as a real-time global
dashboard. The DR and TV 2 destinations are link-only references. Do not copy
their content into the Hub. Review these links before a public release and
after a source changes its channel or URL. The starter-profile language and
regional visibility setting remains a release decision; the current page labels
its Danish services explicitly.

The Danish police list RSS as an official way to receive short operational
updates from the national police, special crime unit, and police districts.
The Hub reads only those fixed official Ritzau feed URLs, caches results for
two minutes, limits response size and item count, and displays retrieval time.
It does not send push notifications. An unavailable feed may show a clearly
labelled cached copy while the Hub still has internet access. The official
Police RSS directory remains linked above; check it for feed changes.
