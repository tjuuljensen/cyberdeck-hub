GETTING STARTED

Build your Cyberdeck

Cyberdeck Hub keeps selected reference material available on your local network, including when the internet is unavailable.

1. Open Admin

Sign in with an administrator account and create cards to group your material by topic.

2. Add offline content

Add documents or ZIM libraries from trusted sources. Review the source, edition, storage needs, and import preview before downloading.

3. Add online links and feeds

Create Online Source or RSS cards for material that still requires an internet connection. These are references to live sources, not offline copies.

4. Back up and maintain

Export configuration and selected content from Admin. Keep origin links and edition details so you can find updated material later.
